# UPDATE 5

## CHAOS MECHANIC
It is new mechanic. There is a tracker of 5 levels, however each step has 2 steps, it goes like this 0,0,1,1,2,2,3,3,4,4,5
We reach new level of chaos after completing 2n steps. And we need to do 2 missions on that new step to advance. There is no 5tier step, but once we complete 2x 4level step we are reaching level 5. 
Clarification: we do 2x mission on tier 0, and reach chaos 1, we do 2x mission of tier 1 and reach chaos 2, we do 2x mission on tier 2 and reach level 3... we assume there was no natural chaos drop. 
There is 11 steps, the last one serves to keep chaos high all the time. 
At the end of turn, wne we roll for alert drop we also roll 5% chaos drop by 1 step. 

### Chaos Effect 
Chaos serves as distraction to police, and reduce effective alertness. So Effective alertnes is max(0, (alert-chaos)) Alert cant go negative. If we ramp chaos high enought the police will be busy, and will not intervenie in our operations. 

### Chaos Mission
Actually there is only 1 mission but it takes many forms. 
Risk is always at least 1, even at tier 0, and then it is just current chaos level. 
To determine mission we first pick current chaos:
0 - Distruption
1 - Decadence
2 - Distrust
3 - Dismantle
4 - Demolishion
5 - Destruction 
We pick risk according to current chaos level at moment. 
it could happen that mission appears but chaos level changes one way or another, oh well, it happens, whatever
Give Chaos Mission short timer like 5turns, you have one shot, one opportunity, take it or loose it. 

And then we pick at random type:
Sneak - arm rebels
Charm - propaganda
Power - sabotage
Org - corruption 

So we can roll Distrust: Arm Rebels for Sneak 2 at Chaos Level 2. 

## DOOMCLOCK
Another new mechanic. Doomclock starts from 0% and progress to 100%. At speed of max (1, (alert-chaos))% . Once it reaches 100% police raid OUR LAIR! Preposterous. 
So far we have two options when this happens:
A) Let them seize you, my friend in capital will hear about it
B) Move the hideout, but we loose our buildings. 
What we loose:
- Cantinas
- Monsters RIP
- Arena
- Spaceport, palace, Bestiary Library
What we keep:
- friends we make along
