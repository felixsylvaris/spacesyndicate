## NEW MECHANIC: SERIOUS WOUND
Each time the team takes a wound (loses health) there is a risk of taking serious wound instead. 
First we need to take wound by normal rules, for example hazard mission. 
Then we role 10%(Risk-2) which means only risk 3+ have serious problem. In case of police pursuit we use unmodified Alert. The more alert police is the heavier weapons they use. 
Serious wound is represented as broken hard (empty) and cant be healed by normal rest. We can still rest for rest bonus, or healing normal wounds. 

### Nanities
We can buy **Nanities** for 125c, and sell them 50c. 
If we have Nanities, rest works on serious wounds, and 1 nanities is takeon from out stash.
