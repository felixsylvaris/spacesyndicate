## Items 
Items are new concept, they are generally 1 use equipment for our agents, some may have cooldown (once per 10turn or something). Items sit in the item deck, if we do acquire item mission but deck is empty we get 20c, or other amount. 1 Hero can use 1 item at the time, but 2 heroes can use 2 (1 each)
We use item by attaching it to agent, it will work for next mission, and then come back to item deck. 
**RETURN** this keyworld on item makes it turn back to deck. 
**COOLDOWN N** This item will go on cooldown for n turns after use, but we keep it. 
**ONE TIME** This item is gone after use, does not turn back to item deck. 

*Note* There is only 4 item so far, dont be dramatic over it.
